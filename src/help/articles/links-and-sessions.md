## Every view is a link

The page address includes the **domain**, the **date range** and any **filters**. That means:

- **Reload** keeps your view.
- **Back** goes to the previous view, not a reset page.
- **Sharing** the link opens the same view for a colleague (with access to the domain).

## Switching products

Switching between the CMP and Analytics keeps the domain and the date range. Pages that exist in both — like Get started — open their counterpart; related pages link to each other under **Related**.

## Signed out?

When your session expires you're asked to sign in again, and afterwards you land on the page you were on — with the same domain, dates and filters.
