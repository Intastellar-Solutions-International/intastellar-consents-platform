**Get started** takes you from a new account to a working setup in one list. Every step ticks itself off once your own data shows it's done — you can't skip one by accident.

## The steps

1. **Add your domain** — shared by both products.
2. **Install on your site** — one piece of code for the banner and, once it's on, Analytics. See [Installing the code](help:install-code).
3. **Consent:** wait for the first recorded consent, verify that you own the domain, run a first cookie scan and create a first report.
4. **Analytics:** turn it on for your domain (one click — this creates the site key), then wait for the first visit.

Connecting an ad platform and defining a conversion are **optional** — they add ad spend and conversion numbers, but setup counts as complete without them.

> Until setup is complete, both Home pages show a small **Setup** card with your progress and the next step.
