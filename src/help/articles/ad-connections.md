Connections are **per domain**: pick the domain, then **Connect** a platform and sign in. If your login has several ad accounts, you choose which one belongs to the domain.

## Sync status

Each connected platform shows its last sync:

- **Synced … · N days added** — all good.
- **Synced … · no data returned** — the platform answered but had no data for the missing days (for example no spend).
- **Last sync failed …** — with the platform's error. Fix the cause, then press **Sync now**.

Data syncs **every night (02:00 UTC)**. **Sync now** fetches missing days immediately.

## Microsoft Advertising

Sign in with the **same Microsoft account you use at ads.microsoft.com**. If one email exists as both a personal and a work/school account, Microsoft asks which one — pick the one with access to your ad accounts.

> A failed sign-in shows the platform's own error message, not just "no ad accounts found".
