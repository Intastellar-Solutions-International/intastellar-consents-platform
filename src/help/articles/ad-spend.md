Ad Spend combines every connected ad platform for the selected domain and period.

## The numbers

- **CTR** — clicks ÷ impressions.
- **CPC** — spend ÷ clicks. Lower is better.
- **CPM** — spend per 1,000 impressions.
- **Blended CAC** — total spend ÷ quality leads (or conversion events when lead quality isn't set up).

Each KPI shows its change against the previous period. For CPC and CAC, a **drop** is shown as good.

## Platforms and campaigns

**Platform comparison** puts spend, share, CTR, CPC and CPM per platform side by side. **Campaigns** are fetched live from the platforms for the selected domain, ranked by spend; campaigns without activity are hidden.

## Currency

Amounts are converted to the currency you pick at the top, with the European Central Bank's daily rates.
