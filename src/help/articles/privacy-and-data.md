## Data stored in the EU

Consent records, analytics data and uploaded files are stored on servers in the **EU**.

## No tracking cookies before consent

The Analytics tracker sets **no cookies** until the visitor consents. Visits without consent are counted **anonymously** — page and consent choice only, without a session — so you still see how much traffic you have.

## What each consent level gives you

- **Full (statistics) consent:** sessions, pages per visit, time on page, sources and conversions.
- **No statistics consent:** anonymous page views and the consent choice.

See also [Banner acceptance vs. statistics consent](help:consent-rates).
