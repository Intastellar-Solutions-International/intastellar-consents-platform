## The source line

Under the main numbers you'll find one quiet line, for example: _Consent records from the cookie banner · Suspected bots excluded · Updated 2 minutes ago_. It says where the numbers come from, which filters apply, and when they were loaded.

## "Too little data"

A change built on fewer than **30** visits, sessions, events or banner decisions shows as **Too little data** instead of a red or green arrow — "3 → 1 users" is not a 67% drop worth worrying about. Hover it to see the counts behind it.

## Comparisons

Changes compare the selected period with the one right before it, of the same length — or with the period you pick under **Compare** in the date range.

## Date range and links

The selected domain, date range and filters are part of the page address. Reloading, the back button and a shared link all keep them. Switching between the CMP and Analytics keeps the date range too. See [Links, sharing and signing in](help:links-and-sessions).
