Live shows the **last 30 minutes** and refreshes every 30 seconds.

- **Active users** counts visitors with **statistics consent** — only they get a session.
- **"+ N events from visitors without full consent"** is everyone else, counted anonymously.
- **Activity per minute** shows all events, so it reflects real traffic even when active users is 0.
- **Countries** are ranked by events, for the same reason.

So _0 active users_ next to activity means people are on the site, just none who allowed statistics cookies.

> Hits from data-centre IPs without consent are hidden by default — see [Suspected bots](help:suspected-bots).
