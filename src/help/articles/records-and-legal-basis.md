## Record of processing activities

**Settings → Record of processing** holds your Article 30 GDPR record — one entry per processing activity.

- **Auto-populate from scan** adds activities for the vendors found in your latest cookie scan.
- **Export** downloads the whole record as a CSV.
- Deleting an entry asks for confirmation — the record is compliance evidence.

## Legal basis

**Settings → Legal basis** sets, per framework and purpose, which legal basis applies, and which services may run on that basis. The banner on your sites is updated when you save. If the settings can't be loaded, saving is disabled so defaults can't replace your configuration.
