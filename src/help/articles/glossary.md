## Consent (CMP)

- **Consent decisions** — banner answers recorded in the period.
- **Banner acceptance rate** — share of decisions that accepted. See [Banner acceptance vs. statistics consent](help:consent-rates).
- **Essential-only** — share of decisions that allowed only necessary cookies.

## Traffic (Analytics)

- **Events** — every page view and tracked event, with or without consent.
- **Active users** — engaged visitors with statistics consent.
- **Sessions** — visits by visitors with statistics consent (others have no session).
- **Bounce rate** — sessions with no engagement (one page, no interaction).
- **Statistics consent rate** — share of events from visitors who allowed statistics cookies.
- **Quality leads** — engaged sessions that reached a page or event you marked as a lead.
- **Conversions** — tracked conversion events (purchase, lead and others you define).

## Ads

- **CTR** — clicks ÷ impressions. **CPC** — spend ÷ clicks. **CPM** — spend per 1,000 impressions.
- **Blended CAC** — total ad spend ÷ quality leads (or conversion events).
