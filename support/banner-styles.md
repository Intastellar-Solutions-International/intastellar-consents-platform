# Banner Styles Support Page

Welcome to the Banner Styles support page! Here you can find descriptions and visual examples of each available banner style, so you can easily choose the one that best fits your needs.

---

## 1. Classic Banner
**Description:**
A traditional horizontal banner that appears at the top or bottom of your website. Ideal for simple, unobtrusive notifications.

![Classic Banner Example](./images/banner-classic.png)

---

## 2. Centered Modal
**Description:**
A modal window that appears in the center of the screen, drawing the user's attention. Great for important messages or consent dialogs.

![Centered Modal Example](./images/banner-centered-modal.png)

---

## 3. Bottom Bar
**Description:**
A slim bar that sticks to the bottom of the page. Perfect for cookie consent or quick alerts.

![Bottom Bar Example](./images/banner-bottom-bar.png)

---

## 4. Floating Box
**Description:**
A small, movable box that floats in a corner of the screen. Allows users to interact or dismiss easily.

![Floating Box Example](./images/banner-floating-box.png)

---

## 5. Full Page Overlay
**Description:**
Covers the entire page with a semi-transparent overlay and a message box. Ensures the user sees the message before proceeding.

![Full Page Overlay Example](./images/banner-full-overlay.png)
